The marking criteria for the Group Report are:  

Problem Understanding: To what extent have you understood and explained the goals of the project, taking account of end-user requirements (15 marks).

Data Wrangling and Exploration: To what extent has extraction, preparation and manipulation of the data been robust, systematic and sophisticated; Depth of insights gained through the initial exploration of the data (15 marks).

Data Modelling, Visualisation, Analysis and Evaluation: Effectiveness of tools chosen and deployed; Appropriateness of the modelling approach; Soundness of machine learning methods applied; Sophistication of the visualisations employed; Effectiveness and insight of the evaluation (40 marks).

Critical Evaluation, Discussion and Conclusion: Consideration of the project’s strengths and weaknesses; Utility of the project findings for the end user (15 marks).
Presentation: Fluency and coherence of the written text; Quality of the figures, charts, graphs, etc. (15 marks).
